# Code of Conduct

## Purpose and scope

Gantt Elastic is a place to build and discuss a Vue Gantt chart. This code applies to project spaces, including issues and
pull requests, and to public situations where someone is acting as a project representative.

## Participation

Participants are expected to:

- Discuss ideas and behavior respectfully, including when giving or receiving technical feedback.
- Make room for different backgrounds, experience levels, and viewpoints.
- Credit work and sources that are not their own.
- Respect personal boundaries and keep private information private.
- Take responsibility for the effects of their contributions and work to repair harm when it occurs.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, unwanted sexual attention, personal attacks, doxxing, impersonation, and repeated
disruptive or abusive behavior are not acceptable. Sharing promotional material unrelated to a project discussion is
also not acceptable.
